Privacy notice

How we handle the personal data you leave us when you book a table. Drawn up under Articles 13 and 14 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR). This English text is a courtesy translation: in case of any difference, the Italian version prevails.

1. Data controller

The data controller is [TO BE COMPLETED: registered company name], with registered office at Via Luigi Pirandello 2r, 17100 Savona (SV), VAT number [TO BE COMPLETED].

For any request about your data you can write to privacy@x-sapori.it or call 019 221 3138.

2. What data we collect

Through the booking form we collect only:

  • date, time and number of guests of the booking;
  • the set menu chosen and the type of occasion;
  • first name, last name and phone number;
  • email address, if you choose to give one (optional field);
  • any notes about allergies, intolerances or preferences.

We do not collect your IP address. To limit automated spam we keep only an irreversible cryptographic code derived from it, from which the original address cannot be reconstructed.

3. Why we process it and on what legal basis

Managing the booking — to contact you, confirm the table and prepare the room. Legal basis: steps taken at the request of the data subject prior to entering into a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Allergies and intolerances — if you write them in the notes we use them to prepare safe dishes. These are health data (Art. 9 GDPR): we process them only on the basis of your explicit consent, given by filling in that field voluntarily, and we pass them on to the kitchen only.

Form security — the code derived from the IP address exists to prevent mass submission of false requests. Legal basis: the controller's legitimate interest in protecting its own site (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

We do not use your data to send you advertising and we do not pass it to third parties for marketing.

4. Who can see the data

Booking requests are accessible to authorised floor and kitchen staff. The request is also sent by email to the restaurant’s mailbox. The only external parties involved are:

  • [TO BE COMPLETED: hosting provider name], which hosts the site, the database and the restaurant mailbox, acting as data processor.

We do not use external messaging services and we do not transfer your data outside the European Economic Area.

5. How long we keep it

We keep booking data for 24 months from the date it is sent, after which it is deleted. That period lets us recognise regular guests and reconstruct any dispute; beyond it we have no need for the data.

6. Your rights

At any time you may ask to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict its processing, receive it in a readable format, or object to processing based on legitimate interest (Arts. 15–22 GDPR). Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing already carried out.

Write to privacy@x-sapori.it: we reply within one month. If you believe the processing breaches the Regulation, you may lodge a complaint with the Italian data protection authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.

7. Cookies

This site uses no profiling cookies and no third-party analytics. The booking form works without cookies. The typefaces are hosted on our own server: no request is sent to Google Fonts.

The home page shows a map. It is not a Google map and it is not embedded from any external service: it is made of OpenStreetMap images hosted on this same site. Looking at it sends no request to any third party, sets no cookie and discloses your IP address to nobody — which is why you are not asked for any consent. The "Directions" button opens Google Maps in a new tab, but only if you press it.
