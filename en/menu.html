Allergies and intolerances

We prepare gluten-free, shellfish-free and nut-free dishes at a separate station. For coeliac guests we ask for at least one day of notice.

The list of allergens in each dish is available: ask the staff in the dining room, or mention the allergy in the notes when you book a table.
