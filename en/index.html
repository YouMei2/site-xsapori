How it works

You help yourself, and every so often we come to you

67 dishes · 8 sections · 4 kitchens

It is a buffet, not an à la carte restaurant: nothing to order and nothing to wait for. Take a plate, walk round the counter, go back as often as you like. Every so often we come round the tables with something just out of the kitchen.

  1. You sit down

    The table is yours. Nothing to write down, nobody to wait for.

  2. You go to the counter

    Take a plate and walk round: what you see is what there is, today.

  3. You go back as often as you like

    No limit on helpings and no surcharge. The price stays the one set at the start.

  4. Every so often we come round

    Grilled meat carved at the table, pineapple, a dessert: things the counter does not have.

How long it lasts: at dinner the table is yours for the whole evening, we do not run two sittings. At lunch we hold it for 90 minutes, so people in a hurry can find a seat too.

One rule only: order what you eat. Dishes left untouched are charged, so nothing ends up in the bin.

The room

Dark marble, blue waves, low light

The walls are slabs of dark grey marble, the ceiling is crossed by teal-blue slats that echo the sea two minutes away, and the tables are pale marble edged in brass.

Warm low light, upholstered chairs, real space between the tables: you can talk without raising your voice even when the room is full. First-time guests usually say the same thing — it does not look like an all you can eat.

Frequently asked

The answers we give on the phone

Do I need to book on weekdays?

At lunch there is usually a table without notice. From Friday to Sunday the room nearly always fills up: better to book, especially for tables of six or more.

How long can we stay at the table?

At dinner the table is yours for the whole evening: we do not run two sittings. At lunch we hold it for 90 minutes, so people in a hurry can find a seat too.

How does pricing work for children?

Children under 120 cm pay €9.90 at lunch and €12.90 at dinner, every day. High chairs are available: just mention it in the booking form.

Do you handle allergies and intolerances?

Yes. Write them in the booking notes: we prepare gluten-free, shellfish-free and nut-free dishes at a separate station. For coeliac guests we ask for at least one day of notice.

Can we celebrate a birthday?

Of course. Select "Birthday" in the form: we bring the cake with candles at the end of the meal. For large groups we set up the private room, up to 40 people with 48 hours of notice.

How can we pay?

We accept credit cards, debit cards and phone payments. Paper meal vouchers at lunch only, up to two per person.

Is there room for pushchairs and wheelchairs?

Yes. The entrance is at street level, the whole room is on the ground floor and the aisles are wide. Tell us when you book and we will give you a table near the entrance.

Do you deliver or do takeaway?

Neither. The format only works with dishes served hot at the table, straight out of the kitchen.
